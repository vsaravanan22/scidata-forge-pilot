# SciData-Forge correspondence-test pilot

This repository reproduces the pilot of the SciData-Forge correspondence test: a check of whether energy and force labels computed with different electronic-structure methods can be pooled for machine learning, either directly or after a validated correction.

It applies the energy and force stages of the test to 22 closed-shell gas-phase molecules containing H, C, N, and O, including the OC20 gas-phase reference molecules, and compares three DFT method settings.

## Quick start

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
./run_pilot.sh            # uses 2 threads by default
```

The full run takes about 16 minutes on a two-core machine. Every step caches its results, so an interrupted run resumes where it stopped. Delete `structures/` and `results/` to start from scratch.

## What the pilot does

1. **Structures** (`scripts/01_build_structures.py`). Each molecule is built from its SMILES string with RDKit (ETKDG embedding, fixed seed, MMFF pre-optimization). It is then optimized at PBE/def2-SVP in PySCF with the pyberny optimizer. Two displaced copies of every optimized geometry are generated by adding Gaussian noise (σ = 0.05 Å, fixed seed) to every Cartesian coordinate.
2. **Calculations** (`scripts/02_compute.py`). All three methods are evaluated at identical geometries: total energies at the optimized geometries and analytic forces at the displaced geometries.
3. **Analysis** (`scripts/03_analyze.py`). For each method pair, the total-energy difference is modeled in two ways:
   - a **constant offset**, and
   - **per-element offsets**: ordinary least squares, without an intercept, on the numbers of H, C, N, and O atoms.

   Both models are evaluated by **leave-one-family-out cross-validation**. Each fold fits on 16 or 17 molecules and tests on the 5 or 6 molecules of the withheld family. Forces are compared by the mean absolute difference of their Cartesian components and by the mean angle between per-atom force vectors.

### Fixed settings

All settings are in `scripts/settings.py` and were fixed before the analysis.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Electronic structure | Restricted Kohn–Sham DFT, density fitting, integration grid level 3, SCF convergence 10⁻⁹ hartree |
| Methods | PBE/def2-SVP, RPBE/def2-TZVP, B3LYP/def2-TZVP |
| Geometries | Optimized at PBE/def2-SVP; all methods evaluated at the same geometries |
| Energy acceptance threshold | 0.05 eV per molecule (MAE on withheld families) |
| Force reference threshold | 0.05 eV/Å |
| Displacements | 2 per molecule, σ = 0.05 Å |

### Molecules and chemical families

Listed in `data/molecules.csv`:

| Family | Molecules |
|---|---|
| Inorganic references (5) | H₂, H₂O, CO, CO₂, N₂ |

## Results

Produced by `./run_pilot.sh` (see `results/table3.md`):

| Method pair | Energy error, constant offset (eV) | Energy error, per-element offsets (eV) | Energy class | Force difference (eV/Å) | Mean force angle (deg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RPBE/def2-TZVP and B3LYP/def2-TZVP | 0.10 | 0.041 | Comparable after correction | 0.26 | 8 |
| PBE/def2-SVP and RPBE/def2-TZVP | 2.37 | 0.133 | Incompatible | 0.20 | 9 |
| PBE/def2-SVP and B3LYP/def2-TZVP | 2.40 | 0.119 | Incompatible | 0.40 | 15 |

Energy errors are mean absolute errors pooled over all withheld molecules.

**Findings**
- A constant offset fails for every pair.
- Per-element offsets bring two functionals with the same basis set within the 0.05 eV threshold, but not pairs that also differ in basis set.
- Force differences exceed the 0.05 eV/Å reference for every pair, including the pair whose energies can be corrected. Energy and force labels must therefore be validated separately.

Per-fold errors and fitted offsets are in `results/lofo_folds.csv`. For the RPBE/B3LYP pair, the hydrocarbon fold alone has an MAE of 0.054 eV, slightly above the threshold; the pooled MAE is 0.041 eV.

### Scope

This is a method-development experiment on a small molecular set. It does not establish performance on the source collections, and it does not compare plane-wave with Gaussian basis sets.

## Outputs

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `structures/opt/*.xyz` | Optimized geometries (Å) |
| `structures/displaced/*.xyz` | Displaced geometries used for forces (Å) |
